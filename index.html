About us

Marbano is a family winery in Bolnisi, a corner of south-east Georgia where wine has been made since the 8th century BC. Winemaker Levan Bandzeladze is turning that inheritance into a craft of his own, growing several native grape varieties and shaping them with the traditional qvevri method, alongside a small selection aged in barrel.
