About us

Marbano — Bolnisi
Marbano

Our story

Marbano is a family winery located in Bolnisi, a special place in south-east Georgia where wine has been made since the 8th century BC. Winemaker Levan Bandzeladze is developing his passion into a profession, cultivating several grape varieties and shaping them with the traditional qvevri method, alongside a small selection aged in barrel.

Marbano

Our wines

Marbano makes wine with grape varieties from the best-known Georgian wine regions. The grapes are harvested by hand when they are ripe, and the wine-making process is calm, cautious, and well-timed. Every wine is rich in phenolic compounds and tannin — taste it once, and you won't forget it.

Recognitions & Medals

Since 2018, Marbano wines have won double gold, gold, silver and bronze medals at prestigious international competitions including IWSC, Mundus Vini, WinExpo, International Qvevri Wine Competition and many more.
